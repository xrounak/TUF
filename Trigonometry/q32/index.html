Trigonometry & Height n Distance
A building and a tower stand on the same level ground. The building is 20 m tall. From the foot of the building, the angle of elevation to the top of the tower is 60°, and from the top of the building it is 30°. Find the height of the tower.
Q
Building = 20 m. Elevation from foot = 60°, from top = 30°. Find the tower's height.
Step 1
Focus: 60° angle at foot of building
Opposite = H Adjacent = d
tan 60° = Hd
√3 = Hd
H = √3 × d
Step 2
Focus: 30° angle at top of building
Standing on top of the building
Building = 20 m (known)
Not seeing full tower height
Extra height = H - 20
Common mistake: don't take H here!
tan 30° = H-20d
1/√3 = H-20d
Step 3
From the first equation:
d = H√3
Substitute into second equation:
H - 20 = H3
3H - 60 = H
2H = 60
H = 30 m
