The angle of elevation of a 20 m tower from a point on level ground is 45 degrees. Find the distance from the point to the tower.
Q
20 m tower, angle of elevation 45°. Find the distance to the tower.
Opposite = 20 m  ·  Adjacent = x
Given
Opposite Side = 20 m
Adjacent Side = x
Step 1
tan θ = OppositeAdjacent
Step 2
tan 45° = 20x
tan 45° = 1 (standard value)
1 = 20x
Step 3
x = 20 m
Distance to the tower = 20 m
