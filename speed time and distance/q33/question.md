Question:
A boat travels downstream at 30 km/hr and the current speed is 5 km/hr. Find the boat's upstream speed.

Options:
A. 25 km/hr
B. 20 km/hr
C. 18 km/hr
D. 22 km/hr

Correct Option: B

Solution:
Given: Downstream Speed = 30 km/hr, Current Speed = 5 km/hr.

Concept: When the boat moves in the same direction as the river, the current helps the boat move faster, so the speeds add up.
Downstream Speed = Speed of Boat in Still Water + Speed of Current

Step 1: Solve for B (speed of boat in still water).
Let B = speed of boat in still water.
B + 5 = 30
B = 30 - 5
B = 25 km/hr

Step 2: Find the upstream speed concept.
Upstream means the boat moves against the flow of the river, so the current slows the boat down, and the speeds subtract.
Upstream Speed = Speed of Boat in Still Water - Speed of Current

Step 3: Compute the answer.
Upstream Speed = B - Current
Upstream Speed = 25 - 5
Upstream Speed = 20 km/hr

So the upstream speed of the boat is 20 km/hr.
Hence, the correct answer is Option B, 20 km/hr.
