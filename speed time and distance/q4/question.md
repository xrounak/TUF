Question:
A bus covers 168 km at a uniform speed of 56 km/hr. How much time does it take?

Options:
A. 3 hours
B. 2 hours
C. 2.5 hours
D. 3.5 hours

Correct Option: A

Solution:
Given values:
Distance = 168 km
Speed = 56 km/hr

Apply the formula.
Time = Distance / Speed

Putting the values:
Time = 168 / 56
= 3 hours

So the time taken by the bus is 3 hours.

Hence, the correct answer is Option A.
