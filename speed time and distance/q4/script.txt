Now in this problem, we are given that a bus covers 168 kilo-mee-ter at a uniform speed of 56 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the time taken by the bus.

Now first, let us note down the given values.

Distance covered by the bus is 168 kilo-mee-ter.

Speed of the bus is 56 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now we know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us put the given values into the formula.

Time taken by the bus is equal to Total Distance divided by Speed.

That is equal to 168 divided by 56.

After simplifying this,

we get 3 hours.

So the time taken by the bus is "3 hours."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
