Now in this problem, A train runs at 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour without halts, but its average speed with halts drops to 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the stoppage time per hour.

Think of it this way.

In 1 hour without any halts, the train would cover 72 kilo-mee-ter.

But because of halts, it covers only 60 kilo-mee-ter in 1 hour.

The difference, 72 my-nus 60, which is 12 kilo-mee-ter, is the distance the train could have covered if it had not stopped.

Now let us find how much time it takes to cover those 12 kilo-mee-ter at the running speed of 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

We know,

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

So,

Time is equal to 12 divided by 72 hour.

After simplifying,

we get 1 by 6 hour.

Now convert this into minutes.

1 by 6 hour is equal to 60 divided by 6, which is 10 minutes.

So the train stops for "10 minutes" every hour.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
