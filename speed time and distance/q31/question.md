Question:
A train runs at 72 km/hr without halts, but its average speed including halts drops to 60 km/hr. Find the stoppage time per hour.

Options:
A. 12 minutes
B. 15 minutes
C. 10 minutes
D. 8 minutes

Correct Option: C

Solution:
Given: Speed without halts = 72 km/hr, Speed with halts = 60 km/hr.

Step 1: Find the distance lost per hour.
In 1 hour without any halts, the train would cover 72 km.
But because of halts, it covers only 60 km in 1 hour.
Distance lost = 72 - 60 = 12 km.

Step 2: Find the time lost.
This 12 km is the distance the train could have covered (at its running speed) if it had not stopped.
Time = Distance / Speed
Time = 12 / 72 hr
Time = 1/6 hr

Step 3: Convert to minutes.
1/6 hr = 60/6 minutes = 10 minutes.

So the train stops for 10 minutes every hour.
Hence, the correct answer is Option C, 10 minutes.
