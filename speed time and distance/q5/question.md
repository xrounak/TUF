Question:
A scooter is moving at a speed of 54 km/hr. How many metres will it cover in 50 seconds?

Options:
A. 650 m
B. 700 m
C. 800 m
D. 750 m

Correct Option: D

Solution:
Given values:
Speed = 54 km/hr
Time = 50 s

The speed is given in km/hr, but the time is given in seconds. So before using the formula, we must first convert the speed into m/s.

We know that to convert km/hr into m/s, we multiply by 5/18.

So, 54 km/hr = 54 x (5/18) m/s
= 15 m/s

Now that the speed and time are in compatible units, apply the formula.
Distance = Speed x Time
= 15 x 50
= 750 m

So the scooter covers 750 m in 50 seconds.

Hence, the correct answer is Option D.
