Speed of the scooter is 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Time taken is 50 seconds.

So before using the formula, we must first convert the speed into "mee-ter per second".

We know that,

to convert "kilo-mee-ter per hour" into "mee-ter per second", we simply multiply by "5 by 18".

Now let us apply this conversion.

54 kilo-mee-ter per hour is equal to 54 into 5 by 18 , mee-ter per second.

After simplifying this,

we get 15 mee-ter per second.

Now this part is completed.

Since the speed and the time are now in compatible units, we can directly use the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.
