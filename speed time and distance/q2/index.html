Step 1
Speed needed in km/hr, but time is in hr & min
1 hr = 60 min
24 min = 24 ÷ 60 hr = 2/5 hr
+ 1 hr already have
Total Time = 1 + 2/5 = 7/5 hr
Step 2
Average Speed = DistanceTime
= 35 ÷ 75
Dividing by a fraction = multiplying by its reciprocal
35 ÷ 75 = 35 × 57
= 25 km/hr
