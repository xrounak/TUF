Now in this problem, we are given that a cyclist rides 35 kilo-mee-ter in 1 hour 24 minutes.

The question is asking us to find the "average speed" of the cyclist.

Now first, let us note down the given values.

Distance covered is 35 kilo-mee-ter.

Time taken is 1 hour 24 minutes.

But be very sure about one thing.

The speed is asked in "kilo-mee-ter per hour", while the time is given in "hours and minutes".

So before using the formula, we must first convert the time into hours.

We know that,

1 hour is equal to 60 minutes.

So,

24 minutes is equal to 24 divided by 60 hours,

which is equal to 2 by 5 hour.

And we already have 1 hour.

So,

Total Time is equal to 1 pluss 2 by 5.

That is equal to 7 by 5 hours.

Got it? Perfect.

Now we know the basic formula.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 35 divided by 7 by 5.

Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal.

So,

35 divided by 7 by 5 becomes,

35 into 5 by 7.

After simplifying this,

we get 25 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the average speed of the cyclist is "25 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
