Now in this problem, we are given that two cars start from two towns which are 330 km apart.

The speed of the first car is 50 km/h.

The speed of the second car is 60 km/h.

Also, both the cars are moving "towards each other."

The question is asking us to find after how many hours they will meet.

Now let us understand one important concept.

When two objects move "towards each other", the distance between them keeps reducing from both sides.

So, in this case, we do not subtract the speeds.

We add them.

And we add the speeds only because both the cars are moving towards each other.

Got it? Perfect.

Now we know the formula.

Relative Speed is equal to the Sum of their Speeds.

Putting the values,

Relative Speed is equal to 50 plus 60.

This gives us 110 km/h.

Now that we know the relative speed, we can find the time taken to meet.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Relative Speed.

Putting the values,

Time is equal to 330 divided by 110.

After simplifying this,

we get 3 hours.

So, the two cars will meet after "3 hours."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
