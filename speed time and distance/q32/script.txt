Now in this problem, we are given that a vehicle travels 120 kilo-mee-ter at 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Then it travels another 180 kilo-mee-ter at 90 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the "average speed" for the complete journey.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

So first, let us find the time taken for each part of the journey.

We know,

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

For the first part,

Time is equal to 120 divided by 60.

This gives us 2 hours.

 for the second part.

Time is equal to 180 divided by 90.

This also gives us 2 hours.

So the total time is equal to 2 pluss 2 , "4" hours

And, the total distance is equal to 120 pluss 180.

That is equal to 300 kilo-mee-ter.

Now we have everything we need.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 300 divided by 4.

After simplifying this, we get 75 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the average speed for the complete journey is "75 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
