Now in this problem, we are given that two bikes start from the same point.

The speed of Bike A is 28 km/h.

The speed of Bike B is 32 km/h.

Both bikes are moving in "opposite directions."

Also, they keep moving for 2 hours.

The question is asking us to find how far apart they will be after 2 hours.

Now let us assume the distance travelled by Bike A is x km.

And let the distance travelled by Bike B be y km.

Now first, let us understand one important concept.

The bikes are moving in "opposite directions."

Because both bikes are moving away from each other, the distance between them keeps increasing.

So the distance between the two bikes will be the sum of their individual distances from the starting point.

That means,

Distance between the two bikes is equal to x pluss y.

Now let us find the distance travelled by each bike.

We know the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

For Bike A,

x, which is the distance travelled by Bike A, is equal to Speed into Time.

That is,

x is equal to 28 into 2.

So,

x is equal to 56 km.

Now let us find the distance travelled by Bike B.

Again, using the same formula,

y, which is the distance travelled by Bike B, is equal to Speed into Time.

That is,

y is equal to 32 into 2.

After solving this,

y is equal to 64 km.

Now we know both distances.

So the distance between the two bikes is equal to x pluss y.

Putting the values,

Distance is equal to 56 pluss 64.

This gives us 120 km.

So after 2 hours, the two bikes will be "120 km" apart.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option B."
