Two bikes move in opposite directions at 28 km/hr and 32 km/hr. Find the distance apart after 2 hours.
Opposite Directions · t = 2 hr
Given
Speed of Bike A = 28 km/hr
Speed of Bike B = 32 km/hr
Time = 2 hours
Distance by A = x km, by B = y km
Step 1
Bikes move in opposite directions
Moving away from each other ⇒ distance keeps increasing
Distance between bikes = x + y
Step 2
x = 28 × 2
= 56 km
y = 32 × 2
= 64 km
Step 3
Distance apart = x + y
= 56 + 64
= 120 km
