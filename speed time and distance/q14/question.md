Question:
Two bikes start from the same point. The speed of Bike A is 28 km/hr, and the speed of Bike B is 32 km/hr. Both bikes move in opposite directions for 2 hours. How far apart will they be after 2 hours?

Options:
A. 130 km
B. 120 km
C. 100 km
D. 150 km

Correct Option: B

Solution:
Let the distance travelled by Bike A be x km, and the distance travelled by Bike B be y km.

Since the bikes are moving in opposite directions, they move away from each other, so the distance between them keeps increasing. This means the distance between the two bikes will be the sum of their individual distances from the starting point.
Distance between the two bikes = x + y

Step 1: Distance travelled by Bike A
Distance = Speed x Time
x = 28 x 2
x = 56 km

Step 2: Distance travelled by Bike B
y = Speed x Time
y = 32 x 2
y = 64 km

Step 3: Total distance apart
Distance between the two bikes = x + y
= 56 + 64
= 120 km

So after 2 hours, the two bikes will be 120 km apart.
Hence, the correct answer is Option B.
