Speeds of A : B = 5 : 6. A takes 72 min. Find B's time.
Ratio 5 : 6
Given
Ratio of speeds (A : B) = 5 : 6
A's time to cover distance = 72 min
Speed of A = 5x
Speed of B = 6x
Step 1
Distance = Speed × Time
For A: distance = 5x × 72
= 360x
B's distance also = 360x
Step 2
Time = 360x6x
x gets cancelled from numerator and denominator
= 60 minutes
