Now in this problem, we are given that the speeds of A and B are in the rayshio of 5 is to 6.

Also, A takes 72 minutes to cover a fixed distance.

The question is asking us to find how much time B will take to cover the same distance.

Now let us assume the speed of A is 5x.

And the speed of B is 6x.

Now let us find the common distance.

We know the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

For A,

Distance is equal to 5x into 72.

This gives us 360x.

Since both of them cover the same distance, B also has to cover 360x.

Now that we know the distance covered by B, let us find the time taken by B.

Time taken by B is equal to distance covered by B 360x divided by the speed 6x.

Here, x gets cancelled from both the numerator and the denominator.

After simplifying this, we get 60 minutes.

So B takes "60 minutes" to cover the same distance.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option B."
