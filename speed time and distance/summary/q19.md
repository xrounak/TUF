Given:
Bus is 45 km ahead of the car
Car speed = 75 km/hr
Bus speed = 60 km/hr

The bus is already moving, and the car is chasing it. Every hour, both keep moving forward. In 1 hour, the car travels 75 km and the bus travels 60 km, so the actual gap reduces by 75 - 60 = 15 km every hour. This is called the relative speed.

Step 1: Relative speed
Relative Speed = Car's Speed - Bus's Speed
= 75 - 60
= 15 km/hr

Step 2: Time to close the gap (unitary method)
The initial gap is 45 km. Every hour, the car reduces only 15 km of that gap.
In 1 hour, the gap reduces by 15 km.
To reduce a gap of 1 km, it takes 1/15 hour.
Since the total gap is 45 km, the time taken will be 45 x 1/15
= 3 hours

So the car will catch the bus after 3 hours.
Hence, the correct answer is Option A.
