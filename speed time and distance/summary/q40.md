Given: Track = 600 m, Walker A speed = 7 m/s, Walker B speed = 5 m/s, Direction = Opposite directions.

Concept: Since they are moving in opposite directions, they are moving towards each other. Every second, Walker A covers 7 m and Walker B covers 5 m, so together they cover 12 m of the track every second.
Since they started from the same point, to meet again they must together complete one full round of the circular track, which is 600 m.

Step 1: Find the formula and the relative speed.
Time = Total Distance / Relative Speed
Relative Speed = 7 + 5 = 12 m/s

Step 2: Substitute the values to get the answer.
Time = 600 / 12
Time = 50 sec

So the two walkers will meet for the first time after 50 seconds.
Hence, the correct answer is Option A, 50 seconds.
