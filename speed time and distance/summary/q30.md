Given: Train length = 160 m, Bridge length = 200 m, Time = 18 s.

Important concept: When a train crosses a bridge completely, it does not cover only its own length. It has to cover the length of the train plus the length of the bridge.

Step 1: Find the total distance covered.
Total Distance = Train length + Bridge length
= 160 + 200
= 360 m

Step 2: Find the speed in m/s.
Speed = Distance / Time
= 360 / 18
= 20 m/s
(Now convert to km/hr, since the options are in km/hr)

Step 3: Convert the speed to km/hr.
Speed (km/hr) = Speed (m/s) x 18/5
= 20 x 18/5
= 72 km/hr

So the speed of the train is 72 km/hr. Hence, the correct answer is Option C, 72 km/hr.
