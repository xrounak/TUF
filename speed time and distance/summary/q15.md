Given:
Faster cyclist speed = 54 km/hr
Slower cyclist speed = 42 km/hr
Gap = 24 km

Understanding the concept:
In one hour, the slower cyclist moves 42 km, while the faster cyclist moves 54 km in that same hour.
The faster cyclist covers the extra distance, which is 54 - 42 = 12 km.
So in one hour, the gap reduces by 12 km.

Step 1: Unitary method
12 km gap closes in 1 hour
So 1 km gap closes in 1/12 hour
Therefore, 24 km gap closes in 24 x 1/12
= 2 hours

Step 2: Using the relative speed formula
Whenever two objects move in the same direction, use the difference of their speeds. This is called the relative speed.
Relative Speed = 54 - 42 = 12 km/hr

Step 3: Time using the formula
Distance = 24 km, Relative Speed = 12 km/hr
Time = Distance / Relative Speed
= 24 / 12
= 2 hours

So the faster cyclist will overtake the slower cyclist after 2 hours.
Hence, the correct answer is Option B.
