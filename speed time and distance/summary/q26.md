Given: Total Distance = 300 km, Total Time = 5 hr, Running Speed = 75 km/hr.

Step 1: Find the actual running time taken to cover 300 km.
Time = Distance / Speed
Running Time = 300 / 75
Running Time = 4 hours

Step 2: The total journey took 5 hours, but the train was actually moving for only 4 hours, so the remaining time is the stoppage time.
Stoppage Time = Total Time - Running Time
= 5 - 4
= 1 hour

So the total stoppage time is 1 hour. Hence, the correct answer is Option C, 1 hours.
