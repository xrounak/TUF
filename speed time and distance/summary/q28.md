Given: Speed = 72 km/hr, Time = 18 s.

Important concept: To cross a pole, a train travels a distance equal to its own length.

Step 1: Convert the speed into m/s.
Speed (m/s) = Speed (km/hr) x 5/18
= 72 x 5/18
= 20 m/s

Step 2: Use the distance formula. Since the distance covered while crossing the pole equals the length of the train:
Distance = Speed x Time
Length of train = 20 x 18
= 360 m

So the length of the train is 360 m. Hence, the correct answer is Option C, 360 m.
