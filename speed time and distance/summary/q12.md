Given:
Distance = 500 m
Runner A speed = 6 m/s
Runner B speed = 4 m/s
Direction = Towards each other

When two objects move towards each other, the distance between them keeps reducing from both sides, so we do not subtract the speeds, we add them.

Step 1: Relative speed
Relative Speed = 6 + 4 = 10 m/s

Step 2: Time to meet
Time = Distance / Relative Speed
= 500 / 10
= 50 s

So the two runners will meet after 50 seconds.
Hence, the correct answer is Option A.
