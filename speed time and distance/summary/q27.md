Given: Train length = 180 m, Speed = 54 km/hr, Time = 30 s.

Important concept: When a train crosses a platform, it does not cover only its own length. It covers the length of the train plus the length of the platform.

Step 1: Convert the speed into m/s.
Speed (m/s) = Speed (km/hr) x 5/18
= 54 x 5/18
= 15 m/s

Step 2: Find the total distance covered in 30 seconds.
Distance = Speed x Time
= 15 x 30
= 450 m
(This 450 m is train length + platform length together)

Step 3: Solve for the platform length.
Train + Platform = 450
180 + Platform = 450
Platform = 450 - 180
Platform = 270 m

So the length of the platform is 270 m. Hence, the correct answer is Option C, 270 m.
