Now in this problem, we are given that the speed of the boat in still water is 18 km/h.

The speed of the stream is 3 km/h.

The boat has to travel 84 km "downstream."

The question is asking us to find the time taken for this journey.

Now before solving the problem, let us understand this

When a boat moves "downstream", it moves in the same direction as the flowing water.

Because both the boat and the stream are moving in the same direction, the stream helps the boat move faster.

So, for downstream movement, we always "add" the speed of the stream to the speed of the boat.

Be very sure about this rule.

Downstream Speed is equal to Speed of Boat in Still Water pluss Speed of Stream.

Now let us find the downstream speed.

Downstream Speed is equal to 18 pluss 3.

This gives us 21 km/h.

Now this part is completed.

Since we know the distance and the speed, we can find the time.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

Putting the values,

Time is equal to 84 divided by 21.

After simplifying this, we get 4 hours.

So the boat will take "4 hours" to travel 84 km downstream.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option D."
