Now in this problem, we are given that the length of the train is 180 m.

The speed of the train is 54 km/h.

And the train takes 30 seconds to completely pass a platform.

The question is asking us to find the length of the platform.

Now first, let us understand one important concept.

When a train crosses a platform, it does not cover only its own length.

It covers the length of the train "plus" the length of the platform.

Now first, let us convert the speed into m/s.

We know,

Speed in m/s is equal to Speed in km/h into 5 by 18.

Putting the values,

54 into 5 by 18.

This gives us 15 m/s.

Now that we know the speed, we can find the total distance covered in 30 seconds.

We know the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

Distance is equal to 15 into 30.

This gives us 450 m.

Now this 450 m is the total distance covered while crossing the platform.

That means,

Train Length plus Platform Length is equal to 450.

The train length is already given as 180 m.

So,

Platform Length is equal to 450 my-nus 180.

This gives us 270 m.

So the length of the platform is "270 m."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
