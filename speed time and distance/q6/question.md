Question:
A car is moving at 20 m/sec. What is its speed in km/hr?

Options:
A. 66 km/hr
B. 78 km/hr
C. 72 km/hr
D. 60 km/hr

Correct Option: C

Solution:
Given value:
Speed = 20 m/s
Find: Speed in km/hr

We should know one important conversion: to convert m/s into km/hr, we multiply by 18/5 (that is, 3.6).

So, 20 m/s = 20 x (18/5) km/hr
= 72 km/hr

So the speed of the car is 72 km/hr.

Hence, the correct answer is Option C.
