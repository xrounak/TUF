Question:
A tour bus covers 150 km at 75 km/hr, halts for 30 minutes, and returns the same 150 km at 50 km/hr. Find the average speed, including the halt.

Options:
A. 50 km/hr
B. 52 km/hr
C. 56 km/hr
D. 54 + (6/11) km/hr

Correct Option: D

Solution:
Given: Distance (each way) = 150 km, Speed (onward) = 75 km/hr, Halt = 30 min, Speed (return) = 50 km/hr.

Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time.

Step 1: Find the total distance.
The bus travels 150 km onward and 150 km while returning.
Total Distance = 150 + 150 = 300 km

Step 2: Find the time for each leg.
Time = Distance / Speed
Onward: Time = 150 / 75 = 2 hr
Return: Time = 150 / 50 = 3 hr

Step 3: Find the total time, including the halt.
Halt = 30 minutes = 0.5 hr
Total Time = 2 + 3 + 0.5 = 5.5 hr

Step 4: Find the average speed.
Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time
Average Speed = 300 / 5.5
Average Speed = 600/11
Average Speed = 54 + (6/11) km/hr

So the average speed of the bus, including the halt, is 54 and 6/11 km/hr.
Hence, the correct answer is Option D, 54 + (6/11) km/hr.
