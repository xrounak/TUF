Two vans from the same depot, opposite directions, at 38 & 47 km/hr. Find the time to be 255 km apart.
Same depot · opposite · 38 & 47 km/hr
Given
Two vans start from the same depot
Speed of Van 1 = 38 km/hr
Speed of Van 2 = 47 km/hr
Moving in opposite directions
Step 1
Opposite directions → distance keeps increasing
Van 1 = 38 km every hour
Van 2 = 47 km every hour
Relative Speed = Sum of Speeds
= 38 + 47
= 85 km/hr
Step 2
Time = DistanceRelative Speed
= 25585
= 3 hours
