Question:
Two delivery vans leave the same depot in opposite directions. One travels at a speed of 38 km/hr and the other at 47 km/hr. After how many hours will they be 255 km apart?

Options:
A. 3 hours
B. 2 hours
C. 4 hours
D. 5 hours

Correct Option: A

Solution:
Given:
Speed of Van 1 = 38 km/hr
Speed of Van 2 = 47 km/hr
Both vans start from the same depot and move in opposite directions.

When two objects move in opposite directions, the distance between them keeps increasing. Every hour, the first van moves 38 km while the second van moves 47 km, so after one hour the distance between them increases by 38 + 47 = 85 km. This is called the relative speed.

Step 1: Relative speed
Relative Speed = Sum of Speeds
= 38 + 47
= 85 km/hr

Step 2: Time to be 255 km apart
Time = Distance / Relative Speed
= 255 / 85
= 3 hours

So after 3 hours, the two delivery vans will be 255 km apart.
Hence, the correct answer is Option A.
