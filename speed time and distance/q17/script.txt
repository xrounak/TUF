Now in this problem, we are given that two delivery vans start from the same depot.

The speed of the first delivery van is 38 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The speed of the second delivery van is 47 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Both vans move in "opposite directions."

The question is asking us to find after how many hours they will be 255 kilo-mee-ter apart.

Now let us understand one important concept.

When two objects move in opposite directions, the distance between them keeps increasing.

Every hour, the first van moves 38 kilo-mee-ter.

At the same time, the second van moves 47 kilo-mee-ter.

So after one hour, the distance between them increases by 38 plus 47.

That is 85 kilo-mee-ter.

This is called the "relative speed."

so, Relative Speed is equal to 38 plus 47.

That gives us 85 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Got it? Perfect.

Now we need to find the time.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Relative Speed.

which is 255 divided by 85.

After simplifying this,

we get 3 hours.

So after "3 hours", the two delivery vans will be 255 kilo-mee-ter apart.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
