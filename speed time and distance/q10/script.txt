Now in this problem, we are given that a car covers two "equal distances."

The first part is covered at 40 km/h.

The second part is covered at 60 km/h.

The question is asking us to find the "average speed" for the whole journey.

Since both distances are equal, let each distance be x km.

Now we know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

So,

Time taken to cover the first part is the distance covered in the first part, which is x, divided by the speed in the first part, which is 40.

That is x by 40 hours.

Similarly,

Time taken to cover the second part is the distance covered in the second part, which is x, divided by the speed in the second part, which is 60.

That is x by 60 hours.

Therefore,

Total Time is x by 40 pluss x by 60,

which simplifies to x by 24 hours.

The total distance is x pluss x, that is 2x km.

Now we know the average speed formula.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 2x divided by x by 24.

The x gets cancelled,

and we are left with 2 into 24,

which is 48 km/h.

So the average speed for the whole journey is "48 km/h."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option D."
