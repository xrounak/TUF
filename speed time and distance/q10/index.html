Speed, Time and Distance
A car covers two equal distances — the first at 40 km/hr, the second at 60 km/hr. What is the average speed for the whole journey?
Q
40 km/hr
60 km/hr
Average Speed = ?
Equal distances at 40 & 60 km/hr — find the average speed.
Each distance = x km
Given
Speed (Part 1) = 40 km/hr
Speed (Part 2) = 60 km/hr
Distance (each part) = x km
Step 1
Time = DistanceSpeed
Time₁ = x40 = x/40 hr
Time₂ = x60 = x/60 hr
Step 2
Total Time = x/40 + x/60 = x/24 hr
Total Distance = x + x = 2x km
Step 3
Average Speed = Total DistanceTotal Time
= 2xx/24
= 2 × 24 = 48 km/hr
