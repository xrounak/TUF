Now in this problem, we are given that the length of the train is 160 mee-ter.

The length of the bridge is 200 mee-ter.

And the train crosses the bridge completely in 18 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the train.

Now let us understand one important concept first.

When a train crosses a bridge completely, it does "not" cover only its own length.

It has to cover the length of the train as well as the length of the bridge.

So, the total distance covered is,

Length of the train plus Length of the bridge.

Putting the values,

Total Distance is equal to 160 plus 200.

This gives us 360 mee-ter.

Got it? Perfect.

Now that we know the total distance  , and

We know,

Speed is equal to Distance divided by Time.

Putting the values,

Speed is equal to 360 divided by 18.

After simplifying this, we get 20 mee-ter per second.

Now be careful here.

The options are given in "kilo-mee-ter per hour", not in mee-ter per second.

So we must convert the speed.

We know,

Speed in kilo-mee-ter per hour is equal to Speed in mee-ter per second into 18 divided by 5.

Putting the values,

Speed is equal to 20 into 18 divided by 5.

This gives us 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the speed of the train is "72 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
