Now in this problem, we are given that a bus is already 45 kilo-mee-ter ahead of a car.

The speed of the car is 75 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The speed of the bus is 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find how long the car will take to catch the bus.

Now let us understand one important concept.

The bus is already moving, and the car is chasing it.

So every hour, both of them keep moving forward.

In 1 hour,

the car travels 75 kilo-mee-ter.

The bus travels 60 kilo-mee-ter.

So the actual gap reduces by 75 minus 60 which is  15 kilo-mee-ter every hour.

This is called the "relative speed."

Relative Speed is equal to Car's Speed my-nus Bus's Speed.

That is,

75 my-nus 60,

which is 15 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now be very sure about one thing.

The initial gap is 45 kilo-mee-ter.

Every hour, the car reduces only 15 kilo-mee-ter of that gap.

So,

in 1 hour, the gap reduces by 15 kilo-mee-ter.

To reduce a gap of 1 kilo-mee-ter, it takes 1 by 15 hour.

Since the total gap is 45 kilo-mee-ter,

the time taken will be 45 into 1 by 15.

After simplifying this,

we get 3 hours.

So the car will catch the bus after "3 hours."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
