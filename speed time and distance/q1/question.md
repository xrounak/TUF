Question:
A man covers 2.4 km in 18 minutes. What is the man's average speed?

Options:
A. 6 km/hr
B. 7 km/hr
C. 9 km/hr
D. 8 km/hr

Correct Option: D

Solution:
Given values:
Distance = 2.4 km
Time = 18 minutes

The speed is asked in km/hr, but the time is given in minutes. So before using the formula, we must first convert the time into hours.

We know that 1 hour = 60 minutes.
So, 18 minutes = 18 / 60 hr
= 3/10 hr

Now apply the basic formula.
Average Speed = Distance / Time

Putting the values:
Average Speed = 2.4 / (3/10)

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.
= 2.4 x (10/3)
= 8 km/hr

So the average speed of the man is 8 km/hr.

Hence, the correct answer is Option D.
