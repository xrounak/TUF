Now in this problem, we are given that a man covers 2.4 kilo-mee-ter in 18 minutes.

The question is asking us to find the average speed of the man.

Now first, let us note down the given values.

Distance covered is 2.4 kilo-mee-ter.

Time taken is 18 minutes.

But the speed is asked in "kilo-mee-ter per hour", while the time is given in "minutes".

So before using the formula, we must first convert the time into hours.

We know that,

1 hour is equal to 60 minutes.

So,

18 minutes is equal to 18 divided by 60 hours.

After simplifying this,

we get 3 by 10 hour.

Now we know the basic formula.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance covered divided by Total Time taken.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 2.4 divided by 3 by 10.

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

So,

2.4 divided by 3 by 10 becomes,

2.4 into 10 by 3.

After simplifying this,

we get 8 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the average speed of the man is "8 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
