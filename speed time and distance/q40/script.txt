Now in this problem, we are given that the circular track is 600 mee-ter long.

The speed of the first walker is 7 mee-ter per second.

The speed of the second walker is 5 mee-ter per second.

Both start from the same point, but they move in "opposite directions."

The question is asking us to find after how many seconds they will meet for the first time.

Now before solving, let us understand the idea first.

Since they are moving in opposite directions, they are moving towards each other.

Every second, the first walker covers 7 mee-ter.

The second walker covers 5 mee-ter.

So together, they reduce the distance between them by 12 mee-ter every second.

Or simply, every second, together they cover 12 mee-ter of the track.

Now they started from the same point.

To meet again, together they must complete one full round of the circular track.

One full round is 600 mee-ter.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us solve it.

Since together they cover 12 mee-ter every second,

Time is equal to Total Distance divided by Relative Speed.

Here, the Relative Speed is equal to 7 pluss 5, which is 12 mee-ter per second.

Putting the values,

Time is equal to 600 divided by 12.

After simplifying this,

we get 50 seconds.

So, the two walkers will meet for the first time after "50 seconds."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
