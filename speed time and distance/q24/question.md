Question:
For the same route, the times taken by two cars are in the ratio 4 : 5. What is the ratio of their speeds?

Options:
A. 4 : 9
B. 5 : 4
C. 4 : 5
D. 9 : 4

Correct Option: B

Solution:
Given: Time ratio (Car A : Car B) = 4 : 5.

Since both cars travel the same route, the distance covered by both is the same. Let the common distance = x km.
Since the time ratio is 4 : 5, let time taken by Car A = 4y hrs and time taken by Car B = 5y hrs.

Step 1: Apply Speed = Distance / Time to each car.
Speed A = x / 4y
Speed B = x / 5y

Step 2: Find the ratio of their speeds.
Speed ratio = (x / 4y) : (x / 5y)
Divide the first fraction by the second fraction:
= (x / 4y) x (5y / x)
x cancels, y cancels
= 5 / 4

So the speed ratio = 5 : 4. Hence, the correct answer is Option B, 5 : 4.
