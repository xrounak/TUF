Given
Speed = 72 km/hr
Time = 18 s
A train at 72 km/hr crosses a pole in 18 s. Find the train's length.
72 km/hr · 18 s
Step 1
Speed (m/s) = Speed (km/hr) × 5/18
72 × 518
= 20 m/s
Step 2
Distance = Speed × Time
(crossing distance = length of the train)
Length of train = 20 × 18
= 360 m
