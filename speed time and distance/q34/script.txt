Now in this problem, we are given that the downstream speed of a boat is 27 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Also, the speed of the boat in still water is 22 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the upstream speed of the boat.

Now before solving, let us understand one simple concept.

When a boat moves "downstream", the current helps the boat.

So,

Downstream Speed is equal to Speed in Still Water pluss Speed of the Current.

We can also write this as,

Speed of the Current is equal to Downstream Speed my-nus Speed in Still Water.

Now we already know the downstream speed and the speed in still water.

Putting the values,

Speed of the Current is equal to 27 my-nus 22.

This gives us 5 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now that we know the speed of the current, we can find the upstream speed.

Be very sure about one thing.

When a boat moves "upstream", the current moves against the boat.

So,

Upstream Speed is equal to Speed in Still Water my-nus Speed of the Current.

Putting the values,

Upstream Speed is equal to 22 my-nus 5.

This gives us 17 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the upstream speed of the boat is "17 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
