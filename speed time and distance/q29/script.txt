Now in this problem, we are given that a train of length 210 mee-ter crosses a pole in 14 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the train in kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now first, let us understand one important concept.

When a train crosses a pole, it covers a distance equal to its own length.

so, Distance covered is 210 mee-ter.

and Time taken is 14 seconds.

Now we know the formula.

Speed is equal to Distance divided by Time.

Putting the values,

Speed is equal to 210 divided by 14.

After simplifying this, we get 15 mee-ter per second.

But the question is asking for the speed in "kilo-mee-ter per hour", not in mee-ter per second.

So we must convert the unit.

We know,

1 mee-ter per second is equal to 18 by 5 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So,

15 into 18 by 5. is "54 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

So the speed of the train is "54 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
