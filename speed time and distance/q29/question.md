Question:
A train of length 210 m crosses a pole in 14 seconds. Find its speed in km/hr.

Options:
A. 50 km/hr
B. 60 km/hr
C. 54 km/hr
D. 48 km/hr

Correct Option: C

Solution:
Given: Length = 210 m, Time = 14 s.

Important concept: When a train crosses a pole, it covers a distance equal to its own length.
So distance covered = 210 m, time taken = 14 s.

Step 1: Find the speed using Speed = Distance / Time.
Speed = 210 / 14
= 15 m/s

Step 2: The question asks for speed in km/hr, not m/s, so convert the unit.
1 m/s = 18/5 km/hr
Speed (km/hr) = 15 x 18/5
= 54 km/hr

So the speed of the train is 54 km/hr. Hence, the correct answer is Option C, 54 km/hr.
