Now in this problem, we are given that a train covers 300 km in 5 hours, including all the stoppages.

Its running speed is 75 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the total stoppage time.

Now first, let us find the actual running time taken by train to cover 300km.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

Putting the values,

Running Time is equal to 300 divided by 75.

This gives us 4 hours.

Now that we know the actual running time, finding the stoppage time becomes very easy.

The total journey took 5 hours, but the train was actually moving for only 4 hours.

So the remaining time must be the stoppage time.

Stoppage Time is equal to Total Time my-nus Running Time.

That is equal to 5 my-nus 4.

This gives us 1 hour.

So the total stoppage time is "1 hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
