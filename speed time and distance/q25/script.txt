Now in this problem, a delivery van travels for 2 hours at 45 km/hr and then for 3 hours at 60 km/hr.

The question is asking us to find the "average speed" of the whole journey.

Now first, let us find the distance covered in each part.

We know the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

For the first part,

Distance is equal to 45 into 2.

This gives us 90 km.

Now the distance covered in the second part is equal to 60 into 3.

This gives us 180 km.

Now that we know both the distances, we can find the total distance.

Total Distance is equal to 90 pluss 180.

This gives us 270 km.

Now this part is completed.

Let us find the total time taken.

Total Time is equal to 2 hours of first part pluss 3 hours of second part.

This gives us 5 hours.

Now we have everything we need.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 270 divided by 5.

After simplifying this,

we get 54 km/hr.

So the average speed of the delivery van is "54 km/hr."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
