Delivery van: 2 hr at 45 km/hr, then 3 hr at 60 km/hr. Find the average speed.
45 km/hr, 2 hr → then → 60 km/hr, 3 hr
Given
Part 1: Time = 2 hr, Speed = 45 km/hr
Part 2: Time = 3 hr, Speed = 60 km/hr
Step 1
Distance = Speed × Time
Part 1: 45 × 2 = 90 km
Part 2: 60 × 3 = 180 km
Step 2
Total Distance = 90 + 180
= 270 km
Step 3
Total Time = 2 hr + 3 hr
= 5 hours
Step 4
Average Speed = Total DistanceTotal Time
= 2705
= 54 km/hr
