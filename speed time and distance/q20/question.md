Question:
Two trains have speeds in the ratio 7 : 9. The faster train covers 180 km in 2 hours. Find the slower train's speed.

Options:
A. 70 km/hr
B. 64 km/hr
C. 68 km/hr
D. 72 km/hr

Correct Option: A

Solution:
Since the ratio of speeds is 7 : 9, let the speed of the slower train be 7x km/hr and the speed of the faster train be 9x km/hr.

Step 1: Find the faster train's actual speed
Speed = Distance / Time
Speed of faster train = 180 / 2
= 90 km/hr

Step 2: Equate and solve for x
Faster train speed = 9x
9x = 90
x = 10

Step 3: Substitute x to find the slower train's speed
Speed of slower train = 7x
= 7 x 10
= 70 km/hr

So the speed of the slower train is 70 km/hr.
Hence, the correct answer is Option A.
