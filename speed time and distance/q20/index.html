Speed of slower train = 7x
Speed of faster train = 9x
Step 1
Speed = DistanceTime
Speed of faster train = 1802
= 90 km/hr
Step 2
Faster train speed = 9x
9x = 90
x = 10
Step 3
Speed of slower train = 7x
= 7 × 10
= 70 km/hr
