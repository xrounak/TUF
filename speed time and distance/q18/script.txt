Now in this problem, we are given that a person goes from home to office at 54 km/h.

Then the person returns by the same route at 36 km/h.

The question is asking us to find the average speed for the complete round trip.

Now let us assume the distance between the home and the office is x km.

Now let us find the time taken for each part of the journey.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

So, the time taken to go from home to office at 54 km/h is x divided by 54 hours.

Similarly, the time taken to return at 36 km/h is x divided by 36 hours.

Now this part is completed.

Next, let us find the total distance.

The person travels x km while going and another x km while coming back.

So the total distance is x pluss x, which is 2x km.

Now let us find the total time.

Total Time is equal to x divided by 54 pluss x divided by 36.

After simplifying this,

the total time becomes 5x divided by 108 hours.

Now we have everything we need.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 2x divided by 5x divided by 108.

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

So this becomes,

2x into 108 divided by 5x.

Here, x gets cancelled.

After simplifying this,

we get 216 divided by 5, which is 43.2.

So the average speed for the complete round trip is "43.2 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
