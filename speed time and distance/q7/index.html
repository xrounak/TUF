Speed, Time and Distance
A bus has a running speed of 54 km/hr and stops for 8 minutes in every hour. What is its average speed including stops?
Q
STOP
54 km/hr

Given
Running Speed = 54 km/hr
Stop Time = 8 min every hour
Bus runs at 54 km/hr, stops 8 min every hour. Average speed?
Speed = 54 km/hr · Stop = 8 min/hr
Step 1
Every hour: 60 min − 8 min stop
= 52 min running
Step 2
Distance = Speed × Time
Speed = 54 km/hr
Time = 5260 hr
54 × 5260 = 46.8 km
Step 3
In this 1 hr (with the stop):
Distance = 46.8 km
Since Time = 1 hour:
Average Speed = 46.8 km/hr
