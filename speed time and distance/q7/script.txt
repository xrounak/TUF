Now in this problem, we are given that a bus runs at 54 km/hr.

But there is one more important point.

The bus stops for 8 minutes in every one hour.

The question is asking us to find the average speed including these stops.

Now let us understand what this really means.

In every one hour, the bus stops for 8 minutes.

So out of every 60 minutes, it is actually moving for only 52 minutes.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us find the distance covered during these 52 minutes.

We know the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

The speed of the bus is 54 km/hr.

And the time for which it is actually moving is 52 minutes which is 52 by 60 hours.

So,

Distance covered is equal to speed into time which is 54 into 52 by 60.

After simplifying this,

we get 46.8 km.

Now think about what we have found.

In one complete hour, including the 8-minute stop, the bus manages to travel only 46.8 km.

And average speed simply means,

"How much distance do we cover in one full hour?"

Since the bus covers only 46.8 km in one complete hour,

its average speed including stops is "46.8 km/hr."

Hence, the correct answer is option "D".
