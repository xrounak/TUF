Now in this problem, we are given that two cyclists are moving in the same direction.

The speed of the slower cyclist is 18 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

After 3 hours, the faster cyclist is 27 kilo-mee-ter ahead.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the faster cyclist.

Now let the speed of the faster cyclist be x kilo-mee-ter per hour.

First, let us find how much distance each cyclist covers in 3 hours.

We know the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

The slower cyclist covers,

18 into 3,

which is 54 km, in 3 hours.

The faster cyclist covers,

x into 3,

which is 3x km, in 3 hours.

Now let us understand the given condition.

After 3 hours, the faster cyclist is 27 kilo-mee-ter ahead.

This simply means that the faster cyclist has covered 27 kilo-mee-ter more than the slower cyclist.

So,

Distance covered by the faster cyclist minus Distance covered by the slower cyclist is equal to 27.

That gives us,

3x minus 54 is equal to 27.

After solving this,

x is equal to 27 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the speed of the faster cyclist is "27 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
