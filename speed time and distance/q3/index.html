A runner maintains a speed of 6 m/s for 12 min. Find the distance.
Speed = 6 m/s · Time = 12 min
Given
Speed = 6 m/s
Time = 12 min
Step 1
Units don't match — convert time
1 min = 60 sec
12 min = 12 × 60 = 720 sec
Step 2
Distance = Speed × Time
= 6 × 720 = 4320 m
Step 3
1000 m = 1 km
4320 ÷ 1000 = 4.32 km
