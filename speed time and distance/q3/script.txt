Now in this problem, we are given that a runner maintains a speed of 6 mee-ter per second for 12 minutes.

The question is asking us to find the total distance covered by the runner.

Now first, let us note down the given values.

Speed of the runner is 6 mee-ter per second.

Time taken is 12 minutes.

Be very sure about one thing.

The speed is given in "mee-ter per second", but the time is given in "minutes".

So before using the formula, we must first convert the time into seconds.

We know that,

1 minute is equal to 60 seconds.

So,

12 minutes is equal to 12 into 60.

That gives us 720 seconds.

Now that the units are the same, we can use the formula.

Distance is equal to Speed into Time.

Putting the values,

Distance is equal to 6 into 720.

After multiplying this,

we get 4320 mee-ter.

Now that we have found the distance in mee-ters, let us convert it into kilo-mee-ter because all the options are given in kilo-mee-ter.

We know that,

1000 mee-ter is equal to 1 kilo-mee-ter.

So,

4320 divided by 1000 is equal to 4.32 kilo-mee-ter.

So the runner covers "4.32 kilo-mee-ter."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option B."
