Question:
A runner maintains a speed of 6 m/sec for 12 minutes. What distance does the runner cover?

Options:
A. 4.8 km
B. 4.32 km
C. 3.6 km
D. 4 km

Correct Option: B

Solution:
Given values:
Speed = 6 m/s
Time = 12 min

The speed is given in m/s, but the time is given in minutes. So before using the formula, we must first convert the time into seconds.

We know that 1 min = 60 sec.
So, 12 min = 12 x 60 = 720 sec

Now that the units match, apply the formula.
Distance = Speed x Time
= 6 x 720
= 4320 m

Since all the options are given in kilometers, convert meters to kilometers.
We know that 1000 m = 1 km.
So, 4320 / 1000 = 4.32 km

So the runner covers 4.32 km.

Hence, the correct answer is Option B.
