Question:
A train covers 90 km at 60 km/hr, 120 km at 80 km/hr and 150 km at 100 km/hr. Find its average speed.

Options:
A. 75 km/hr
B. 78 km/hr
C. 82 km/hr
D. 80 km/hr

Correct Option: D

Solution:
Given: Part 1: 90 km at 60 km/hr, Part 2: 120 km at 80 km/hr, Part 3: 150 km at 100 km/hr.

Average speed is NOT the average of the three speeds. Always use the formula:
Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time

Step 1: Find the total distance.
Total distance = 90 + 120 + 150 = 360 km

Step 2: Find the time taken for each part using Time = Distance / Speed.
1st part = 90 / 60 = 1.5 hr
2nd part = 120 / 80 = 1.5 hr
3rd part = 150 / 100 = 1.5 hr

Step 3: Find the total time.
Total time = 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 = 4.5 hr

Step 4: Find the average speed.
Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time
= 360 / 4.5
= 80 km/hr

So the average speed of the train is 80 km/hr. Hence, the correct answer is Option D, 80 km/hr.
