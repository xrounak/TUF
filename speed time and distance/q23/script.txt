Now in this problem, we are given that a train covers 90 km at 60 km/h, 120 km at 80 km/h, and 150 km at 100 km/h.

The question is asking us to find the "average speed" for the whole journey.

Be very sure about one thing.

Average speed is "not" the average of the three speeds.

We always use the formula.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Now first, let us find the total distance.

Total Distance is equal to 90 pluss 120 pluss 150.

This gives us 360 km.

Now that we know the total distance, let us find the time taken for each part.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

For the first part,

Time is equal to 90 divided by 60.

This gives us 1.5 hours.

For the second part,

Time is equal to 120 divided by 80.

This also gives us 1.5 hours.

For the third part,

Time is equal to 150 divided by 100.

Again, this gives us 1.5 hours.

Now this part is completed.

so let us find the total time.

Total Time is equal to 1.5 pluss 1.5 pluss 1.5.

This gives us 4.5 hours.

Now , Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 360 divided by 4.5.

After simplifying this, we get 80 km/h.

So the average speed of the train is "80 km/h."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option D."
