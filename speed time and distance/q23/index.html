Speed, Time and Distance
A train covers 90 km at 60 km/hr, 120 km at 80 km/hr and 150 km at 100 km/hr. Find its average speed.
Q
Find: Average Speed = ?
90 km @ 60 km/hr
120 km @ 80 km/hr
150 km @ 100 km/hr
NOT the average of 3 speeds!
Avg Speed = Total Dist ÷ Total Time
Three segments — 90@60, 120@80, 150@100 km/hr — find its average speed.
90, 120, 150 km journey
Given
Part 1: 90 km @ 60 km/hr
Part 2: 120 km @ 80 km/hr
Part 3: 150 km @ 100 km/hr
Step 1
Total distance = 90 + 120 + 150
= 360 km
Step 2
Time = DistanceSpeed
1st part = 9060 = 1.5 hr
2nd part = 12080 = 1.5 hr
3rd part = 150100 = 1.5 hr
Step 3
Total time = 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5
= 4.5 hr
Step 4
Avg Speed = Total Dist ÷ Total Time
Putting the values:
= 360 ÷ 4.5
= 80 km/hr
