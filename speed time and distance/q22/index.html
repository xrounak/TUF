Speed, Time and Distance
Excluding stoppages, a bus runs at 60 km/hr. Including stoppages, its average speed is 45 km/hr. How many minutes does it stop per hour?
Q
❚❚
?
Running: 60 km/hr
Avg (with stops): 45 km/hr
Excl. stoppages: bus runs at 60 km/hr. Incl. stoppages: avg = 45 km/hr. Find stoppage time/hour.
❚❚
Excl: 60 → Incl: 45 km/hr
Given
Speed (no stops) = 60 km/hr
Avg speed (with stops) = 45 km/hr
Step 1
If the bus never stopped for 1 hour…
Distance = 60 km/hr × 1 hr = 60 km
Step 2
But with stoppages, avg speed = 45 km/hr
So distance in 1 hr = 45 km
Missed distance = 60 − 45 = 15 km
Step 3
Time to cover the missed 15 km?
Time = Distance / Speed
= 15 / 60 hr
= 1/4 hr = 15 min
