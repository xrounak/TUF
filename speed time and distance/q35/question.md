Question:
A boat's speed in still water is 20 km/hr and the current speed is 4 km/hr. Find the time needed to cover 64 km upstream.

Options:
A. 3 hours
B. 3.5 hours
C. 4.5 hours
D. 4 hours

Correct Option: D

Solution:
Given: Still Water Speed = 20 km/hr, Current Speed = 4 km/hr, Distance = 64 km (upstream).

Concept: When a boat moves upstream, it moves against the flow of the river, so the current slows the boat down.
Upstream Speed = Still Water Speed - Current

Step 1: Find the upstream speed.
Upstream Speed = 20 - 4
Upstream Speed = 16 km/hr

Step 2: Find the time.
Time = Distance / Speed
Time = 64 / 16
Time = 4 hours

So the time needed to cover 64 km upstream is 4 hours.
Hence, the correct answer is Option D, 4 hours.
