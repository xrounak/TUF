Now in this problem, we are given that the speed of the boat in still water is 20 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Also, the speed of the current is 4 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the time needed to cover 64 kilo-mee-ter "upstream."

Now before solving, let us understand one simple concept.

When a boat moves "upstream", it is moving against the flow of the river.

So, the current slows the boat down.

That is why,

Upstream Speed is equal to Speed of Boat in Still Water my-nus Speed of Current.

Now let us find the upstream speed.

Putting the values,

Upstream Speed is equal to 20 my-nus 4.

After simplifying this,

we get 16 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now this part is completed.

We already know the upstream speed, so now we can find the required time.

We know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

Putting the values,

Time is equal to 64 divided by 16.

After simplifying this,

we get 4 hours.

So, the time needed to cover 64 kilo-mee-ter upstream is "4 hours."
