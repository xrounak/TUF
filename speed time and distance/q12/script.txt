Now in this problem, we are given that two runners are 500 mee-ter apart.

The first runner is running at 6 m/s.

The second runner is running at 4 m/s.

Also, they are running "towards each other."

The question is asking us to find after how many seconds they will meet.

Now let us understand one important concept.

When two objects move "towards each other", the distance between them keeps reducing from both sides.

So, in this case, we do not subtract the speeds.

We add them.

and We add the speeds only because they are moving towards each other.

Got it? Perfect.

So, the Relative Speed is equal to the sum of their speeds.

Relative Speed = 6 + 4 = 10 m/s.

Now we know the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Relative Speed.

Putting the values,

Time = 500 divided by 10.

which is  50 seconds.

So the two runners will meet after "50 seconds."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option A."
