WHAT THE QUESTIONS IN THIS BANK ARE: three named people, two stated links (one sentence each), and
one asked-for link between the two outer people. Example: "Amit is the father of Neha. Neha is the
sister of Karan. How is Amit related to Karan?" The figure is therefore always a three-node graph
with two solid edges and one dashed grey gap, and the video is the act of closing that gap. Real
names on every node, never letters. A fourth node is a design error.


STANDING ITEMS CARRIED OVER FROM Trigonometry/penelty.txt (topic-agnostic, still binding):

1. Clip data-duration buffer (q9 bug): any clip whose data-duration ends exactly at a
   morphToStack / fadeOut / pinFlow call needs +1.5-2s past that transition's completion
   (morph = start+0.8s, fadeOut ~0.4s), or the animation visibly cuts off at unmount.

2. Label clipping (q10 bug): size the SVG viewBox with margin for the full label string at its
   font-size, not just for the geometry. Here that means the NAME text under each node, which is
   wider than the 56px node box, and the relation chips, which are wider still.

3. Exact-copy pinned figure: the Phase-2 pinned icon must be a coordinate-exact copy of the
   full-size figure (same viewBox, same internal numbers) with only outer width/height shrunk.
   Never a separately-eyeballed smaller redraw.

4. stroke-dasharray on any drawn line/path must equal that path's true total length, with the
   arithmetic in a trailing comment. For multi-segment descent paths, sum every segment.

5. Every coordinate used in more than one place is defined ONCE in the comment block at the top
   of the illustration and reused literally. No restating "the same" point with different numbers.

6. No em dash in any file. No bare division character in solution-card math - use .frac.
   No infinite repeat: -1 anywhere.

NOT APPLICABLE IN THIS TOPIC: the angle-arc constitution (--angle custom property, translate(0,2*cy)
flip, atan2 check, angle-vertex-on-the-left rule). There is no arc here. Do not copy that machinery
into a family tree.


TOPIC-SPECIFIC STANDING ITEMS (full text in illustration_reference.md sections 3, 6, 7 and 12):

7. Derive all three genders from the statement's relation words before drawing, and write them in
   the geometry comment. father/brother/son/husband -> square. mother/sister/daughter/wife ->
   circle. Never stated -> rounded square rx=14, and confirm the answer does not depend on it.
   Drawing a gender the question never gave is a factual error in the figure.

8. Name goes UNDER the node, font-size 26, baseline cy+46, same offset on every node. Not inside
   the shape - names are 4-7 characters and either overflow the 56px box or fall below the
   legibility floor. 26 is chosen so the pinned copy lands at 22.4px, clear of the 21px floor.

9. "Brother of" / "sister of" is a SIBLING BAR: both people on the SAME row, joined by a single
   horizontal line with drop stubs. Putting a sibling below their sibling claims a parent link.
   Marriage / in-law links are horizontal DOUBLE lines (10px gap). Descent links are vertical or
   square-cornered single lines. No diagonals, no curves.

10. Statement order is NOT top-to-bottom order. "Pooja is the daughter of Harish" names Harish
    second but places him on the row ABOVE. Resolve every direction before assigning rows.

11. The answer edge is the only dashed edge in the figure. Grey (#949494) with a "?" while
    unresolved, and it must clear every node box by >= 20px - route it around the outside with a
    square corner. In a three-generation question it passes the middle person; if it grazes their
    node the figure reads as if the relation runs through them. It resolves dashed-to-solid plus
    grey-to-green in ONE 0.5s tween. Never fade a finished green line in instead.

12. Maternal vs paternal is the answer in some questions ("Dev is the son of Kavita. Kavita is the
    sister of Manoj." -> maternal uncle). The mother's-side path must be unmistakable in the
    figure: sibling bar above the mother, pivot fill on the mother.

13. The answer word's gender follows the ANSWERING person, not the pivot ("Anita is the wife of
    Rohit. Rohit is the father of Meera." -> mother, not father). Check the answer word against
    the answering node's shape before shipping.

14. Every relation chip carries the exact lower-case word the statement used (father, sister,
    daughter, wife, son, brother, mother, husband). No paraphrase, no symbol. Chip and narration
    say the same word on the same frame.

15. Phase 1 needs >= 8 timed events across the two-sentence statement (3 nodes, 2 edges, 2 chips,
    endpoint highlights, answer edge). Fewer means chips or the answer edge were skipped.
