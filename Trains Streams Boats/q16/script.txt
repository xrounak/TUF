Now in this problem, we are given that a train is travelling at 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour, and it crosses a pole in 15 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the length of the train.

Now the important words here are "crosses a pole."
<break time="0.5s" />

What does this actually mean?

A pole has almost no width.

So when a train crosses a pole, the train covers only its own length.

This is a simple rule to remember.

When a train crosses a pole, Distance Covered = Length of the Train.

Done? Great.

Now before using the formula, we should notice one more thing.

The speed is given in kilo-mee-ter per hour, but the time is given in seconds.

So first, we must convert the speed into mee-ter per second.

We know,

72 multiplied by 5 divided by 18 is equal to 20 mee-ter per second.

Now this part is done. We can use this value in the next step.

We know,

Distance = Speed multiplied by Time.

Since the distance covered is the length of the train,

Length = 20 multiplied by 15.

After multiplying, we get 300 mee-ter.

<break time="0.6s" />

Hence, the length of the train is "300 mee-ter."
