Q
Distance = Train's Length
GIVEN
Speed = 72 km/h
Time = 15 s (pole)
Find: Length?
Train at 72 km/h crosses a pole in 15 s. Find length.
Speed = 72 km/h, Time = 15 s
Given
Speed = 72 km/h
Time = 15 s (crosses pole)
Distance = Length of Train
Step 1
Convert speed into m/s:
72 × 518 = 20 m/s
Step 2
Distance = Speed × Time
Length = 20 × 15
= 300 m
