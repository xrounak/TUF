Now in this problem, we are given that the speed of the boat in still water is 12 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Also, the speed of the stream is 3 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the "downstream speed" of the boat.

Now before solving, let us understand one simple concept.

Imagine the river is flowing in one direction.

When the boat moves in the "same direction" as the river, the stream helps the boat move faster.

So both the speeds get added.

This is the basic rule to remember.

Downstream Speed is equal to Speed in Still Water pluss Speed of the Stream.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us put the given values into the formula.

Downstream Speed is equal to 12 pluss 3.

After adding them,

we get 15 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the downstream speed of the boat is "15 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option B."
