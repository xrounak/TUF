Now in this problem, two trains start from two cities which are 420 kilo-mee-ter apart.

One train is moving at 60 km/h, and the other train is moving at 80 km/h.

The question is asking us to find after how many hours the two trains will meet.

Now the important point here is that both trains are moving "towards each other". <break time="0.5s" />

So the distance between them is getting reduced from both sides at the combined rate of their speeds.

Therefore, when two bodies move towards each other, their relative speed is the sum of their speeds.

Relative Speed = 60 pluss 80 = 140 km/h.

Done. Now we have everything we need.

We know,
Distance = 420 kilo-mee-ter

Relative Speed = 140 km/h

Now we can use the Speed–Distance–Time formula.

Time = Distance divided by Relative Speed.

Substituting the values,

Time = 420 divided by 140.

After simplifying this, we get 3 hours.

<break time="0.6s" />

So the trains will meet after "3 hours".

Hence, the correct option is "Option B".
