Now in this problem, we are given that a train is 180 mee-ter long and it is running at 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The train crosses a pole.

The question is asking us to find how many seconds it takes.

Now before solving, let us understand one simple concept.

When a train crosses a "pole", the pole has almost no width to cover.

So the train only has to cover its "own length".

Now let us write the given values.

Length of the train is 180 mee-ter.

Speed of the train is 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So first, we should convert the speed into mee-ter per second.

We know that,

1 kilo-mee-ter per hour is equal to 5 by 18 mee-ter per second.

So,

54 into 5 by 18 gives us 15 mee-ter per second.

Now this part is completed.

We already know the distance and the speed, so let us use the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

Putting the values,

Time is equal to 180 divided by 15.

After simplifying this, we get 12 seconds.

So the train takes "12 seconds" to cross the pole.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
