Now in this problem, we are given a train of length 140 mee-ter, a bridge of length 220 mee-ter, and the speed of the train is 18 mee-ter per second.

The question is asking us to find how much time the train takes to completely cross the bridge.

Now the important point here is "completely cross the bridge."
<break time="0.5s" />

What does this actually mean?

It means the last compartment of the train should also come out of the bridge.

So the train has to cover its own length as well as the length of the bridge.

This is a simple rule to remember.

Whenever a train crosses a bridge,

Total Distance = Length of Train + Length of Bridge.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us put the given values.

Length of train = 140 mee-ter.

Length of bridge = 220 mee-ter.

So the total distance becomes 140 pluss 220, which is 360 mee-ter.

Now this part is done. We already know the total distance.
The remaining work is very easy.

We know,

Time = Distance divided by Speed.

Putting the values,

Time = 360 divided by 18.

After solving this, we get 20 seconds.
<break time="0.6s" />

Hence, the train takes "20 seconds" to completely cross the bridge.

So the final answer will be Option A.
