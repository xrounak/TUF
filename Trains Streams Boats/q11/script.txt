Now in this problem, we are given that two trains are moving in "opposite directions."

The speed of the first train is 45 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The speed of the second train is 55 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find their "relative speed."

Now before using the formula, let us understand one simple concept.

Relative speed tells us how fast one object is moving with respect to the other.

When two trains move in the "same direction", we subtract their speeds.

But when they move in "opposite directions", they come towards each other.

So their speeds get added.

This is a direct formula-based question.

Relative Speed is equal to the sum of both speeds.

Now let us put the given values.

Relative Speed is equal to 45 pluss 55.

After adding them,

we get 100 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the relative speed of the two trains is "100 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
