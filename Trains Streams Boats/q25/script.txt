Now in this problem, we are given that a boat covers 48 kilo-mee-ter upstream in 4 hours.

Also, the speed of the stream, or current, is 3 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the boat in still water.

Before solving, let us take the boat's speed in still water as B kilo-mee-ter per hour, and the stream speed as C kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now the important point here is "upstream".

What does upstream mean?

When the boat moves upstream, the water pushes against the boat.

So the boat becomes slower.

That is why,

Upstream Speed = Boat Speed in Still Water minus Stream Speed.

So we can write,

B minus C.

Done. Now let us find the upstream speed first.

We know,

Speed = Distance divided by Time.

So,

48 divided by 4 gives us 12 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now that we have found the upstream speed, we can use it in the formula.

We already know,

B minus C = 12.

And the stream speed C is 3.

So,

B minus 3 equals 12.

After solving this, we get,

B equals 15 kilo-mee-ter per hour.
