Now in this problem, we are given that a train is 200 mee-ter long and it is running at 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The train crosses a platform in 30 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the length of the platform.

Now the important words here are "crosses a platform."
<break time="0.5s" />

What does this actually mean?

When a train crosses a platform, it does not cover only the platform.

The entire train has to enter the platform and then completely come out of it.

So the total distance covered is,

Length of the Train pluss Length of the Platform.

This is the basic concept to remember.

Now let us assume the length of the platform is **x** mee-ter.

Before using the formula, let us convert the speed into mee-ter per second.

54 multiplied by 5 divided by 18 gives us 15 mee-ter per second.

Done. Now we have all the values we need.

We know,

Distance = Speed multiplied by Time.

So,

Distance = 15 multiplied by 30 = 450 mee-ter.

Now that we have found the total distance, we can use it in the next step.

Since,

Length of Train pluss Length of Platform = Total Distance,

we get,

200 pluss x = 450.

After simplifying this, we get,

x = 450 my-nus 200,

which is 250 mee-ter.

<break time="0.6s" />

Hence, the length of the platform is "250 mee-ter."
