Now in this problem, we are given that a boat covers 42 kilo-mee-ter upstream at a speed of 14 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the time required for the journey.

Now first, let us note down the given values.

Distance travelled upstream is 42 kilo-mee-ter.

Upstream speed of the boat is 14 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now we know the basic formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

Putting the values,

Time is equal to 42 divided by 14.

After simplifying this,

we get 3 hours.

So the boat takes "3 hours" to travel 42 kilo-mee-ter upstream.

Hence, the correct answer is "Option D."
