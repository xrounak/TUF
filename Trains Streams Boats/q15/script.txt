Now in this problem, we are given that two trains are moving in the same direction.

The speed of the first train is 70 kilo-mee-ter per hour, and the speed of the second train is 52 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find their "relative speed."

Now the important words here are "same direction."
<break time="0.5s" />

What does this mean?

It means both trains are moving one behind the other.

So one train is not coming towards the other. Instead, one train is only catching up with the other.

Because of this, we do not add the speeds.

We always take the "difference" of their speeds.

This is a simple rule to remember.

If two objects move in the same direction, Relative Speed = Difference of their speeds.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us put the given values.

Relative Speed = 70 my-nus 52.

After subtracting, we get 18 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

<break time="0.6s" />

Hence, the relative speed of the two trains is "18 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
