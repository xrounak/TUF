Now in this problem, a boat travels 24 kilo-mee-ter upstream at 8 km/h, and then 36 kilo-mee-ter downstream at 12 km/h.

The question is asking us to find the "total time" taken for the whole journey.

Now the important point here is that the journey has two different parts.
One part is upstream, and the other part is downstream.
So we cannot calculate the total time directly.
We first find the time for each part separately, and then add them.
<break time="0.5s" />

Given,

Upstream Distance = 24 kilo-mee-ter

Upstream Speed = 8 km/h

Downstream Distance = 36 kilo-mee-ter

Downstream Speed = 12 km/h

Now we know the formula for time.

Time = Distance ÷ Speed

So let us use this formula one by one.

First, the time taken upstream.

Time = 24 ÷ 8 = 3 hours.

Done. Now let us move to the downstream journey.

Time = 36 ÷ 12 = 3 hours.

Now that both parts are clear, we simply add them because the boat completes both journeys one after the other.
<break time="0.5s" />

Total Time = 3 + 3 = 6 hours.
<break time="0.6s" />

Hence, the total time taken is "6 hours".

So the correct option is "Option C".
