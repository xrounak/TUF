Log at 4 km/h with current, boat still-water 12 km/h. Find upstream speed.
Boat Speed(still Water) = B
Current Speed = C
Given
Speed of floating log = 4 km/h
Boat's speed in still water = 12 km/h
Step 1
A floating log has no engine, it simply moves with the current
c = 4 km/h
Step 2
Upstream moves against the current
Upstream Speed = b - c
= 12 - 4
= 8 km/h
