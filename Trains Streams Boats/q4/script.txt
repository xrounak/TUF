Now in this problem, we are given that the speed of the boat in still water is 18 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Also, the speed of the stream is 4 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the "upstream speed" of the boat.

Now before using the formula, let us understand the concept.

When a boat moves "upstream", it is moving against the flow of the river.

So the stream slows the boat down.

That is why we subtract the speed of the stream from the speed of the boat in still water.

The formula is,

Upstream Speed is equal to Speed in Still Water my-nus Speed of Stream.

Now let us put the given values.

Upstream Speed is equal to 18 my-nus 4.

After simplifying this,

we get 14 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the upstream speed of the boat is "14 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
