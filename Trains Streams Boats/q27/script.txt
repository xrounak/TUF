Now in this problem, a boat travels 36 kilo-mee-ter downstream and 36 kilo-mee-ter upstream.

The speed of the boat in still water is 15 kilo-mee-ter per hour, and the speed of the stream is 3 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

We need to find the total time taken for the whole journey.

Now before solving, let us quickly recall one basic concept.

The boat's speed in still water means the speed when there is no current.

The stream helps the boat while moving downstream, so the speeds get added.

And the stream slows down the boat while moving upstream, so the speeds get subtracted.

This is the main idea of the question.

So first, let us find both speeds.

Downstream speed = speed of boat + stream speed which is 15 + 3 = 18 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Upstream speed = speed of boat - stream speed which is 15 - 3 = 12 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Done. Now we have both speeds.

The formula we need is:

Time = Distance divided by Speed.

Now that we have the speeds, finding the time becomes easy.

Time taken downstream = 36 divided by 18 = 2 hours.

Time taken upstream = 36 divided by 12 = 3 hours.

Now only one step is left.

The total time is 2 + 3 = 5 hours.
