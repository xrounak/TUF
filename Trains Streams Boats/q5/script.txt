Now in this problem, we are given that the speed of a train is 25 mee-ter per second.

The question is asking us to find its speed in "kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Now before solving, let us remember one simple conversion.

1 mee-ter per second is equal to 18 by 5 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

This is a direct conversion, so we simply multiply the given speed by 18 by 5.

Now let us put the values.

Speed in kilo-mee-ter per hour is equal to 25 into 18 by 5.

Since 25 is divisible by 5, we get,

5 into 18.

After simplifying this, we get 90 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Done and dusted.

So the speed of the train is "90 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
