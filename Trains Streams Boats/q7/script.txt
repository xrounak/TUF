Now in this problem, we are given that the length of the train is 150 mee-ter.

The train crosses a pole in 10 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the train in kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now before solving, let us understand one simple concept.

When a train crosses a "pole", it covers only its own length.

Because a pole has no width.

So the distance covered is simply the length of the train.

Got it? Perfect.

Now let us note down the values.

Distance covered is 150 mee-ter.

Time taken is 10 seconds.

We know the basic formula.

Speed is equal to Distance divided by Time.

Putting the values,

Speed is equal to 150 divided by 10.

This gives us 15 mee-ter per second.

Now be very careful here.

The question is asking for the speed in "kilo-mee-ter per hour", but our answer is in "mee-ter per second."

So we must convert the units.

We know that,

1 mee-ter per second is equal to 3.6 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So,

15 into 3.6 gives us 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the speed of the train is "54 kilo-mee-ter per hour."

Hence, the correct answer is "Option C."
