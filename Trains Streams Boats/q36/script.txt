Now in this problem, we have a train of length 240 mee-ter.

It crosses a platform of length 260 mee-ter in 25 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the train in kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Now the important point here is "crosses a platform".

What does this actually mean?

When a train completely crosses a platform, it has to cover its own length as well as the length of the platform.

So the total distance covered will be,

Train Length pluss Platform Length.

That is,

240 pluss 260 = 500 mee-ter.

Got it? Perfect.

Now that we have the total distance, we can find the speed.

We know,

Speed = Distance divided by Time.

Putting the values,

Speed = 500 divided by 25.

After solving this, we get,

Speed = 20 mee-ter per second.

Now the question is asking for the answer in kilo-mee-ter per hour.

72 kilo-mee-ter per hour.
