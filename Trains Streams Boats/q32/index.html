A train crosses a 150 m and a 250 m platform. Find the speed.
Platform 1 = 150 m / 20 s · Platform 2 = 250 m / 25 s
Given
Platform 1 = 150 m, Time = 20 s
Platform 2 = 250 m, Time = 25 s
Let Train = L m, Speed = S m/s
Step 1
Distance = Train + Platform
Speed = DistanceTime
Step 2
Time = 20 s
S = L + 15020
20S = L + 150
Step 3
Time = 25 s
S = L + 25025
25S = L + 250
Step 4
25S − 20S = (L+250) − (L+150)
5S = 100
S = 20 m/s
