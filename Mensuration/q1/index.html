The area of a square is 144 cm². Find its perimeter.
Q
Area of square = 144 cm². Find perimeter.
Given
Area = 144 cm²
Step 1
Area = s2 = 144
s = √144 = 12 cm
Step 2
Perimeter = 4 × side
(a square has 4 equal sides)
= 4 × 12 = 48 cm
