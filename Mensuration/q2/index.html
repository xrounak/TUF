In a brief shower, 2 cm of rain falls on 2 hectares of ground. The volume of water that falls on the ground is:
Q
2 cm of rain falls on 2 hectares of ground. Find the volume of water.
Given
Rain = 2 cm, Area = 2 ha
Step 1
Convert the area:
1 hectare = 10,000 m²
2 hectares = 20,000 m²
Step 2
Convert the rainfall:
100 cm = 1 m
2 cm = 0.02 m
Step 3
Volume = Area × Height
= 20,000 × 0.02
= 400 m³
Step 4
Final answer: 400 m³
G
Rain = 2 cm, Area = 2 ha
1

2
Height = 0.02 m
3

4
