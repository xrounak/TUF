If the length and breadth of a rectangle increase by 10% and 20% respectively, find the percentage increase in its area.
Q
Area = Length × Breadth
Rectangle: length +10%, breadth +20% - find % increase in area.
Given
Assume Length = 100
Assume Breadth = 100
Step 1
Original Area
= 100 × 100
= 10,000
Step 2
Length increases 10% -> 110
Breadth increases 20% -> 120
Step 3
New Area
= 110 × 120
= 13,200
Step 4
Increase = 13,200 - 10,000
= 3,200
% Increase = 3,20010,000 × 100
= 32%
