50 men take a dip in a water tank 50 m long and 20 m broad. If the average displacement of water by a man is 4 m³, find the rise in the water level.
Q
Tank 50 m x 20 m, 50 men each displace 4 m³. Find the rise.
Given
Men = 50
Length = 50 m
Breadth = 20 m
Displacement/man = 4 m³
Step 1
1 man displaces = 4 m³
50 x 4 = 200 m³
Step 2
Area = Length x Breadth
50 x 20 = 1000 m²
Step 3
Volume = Area x Height
Height = VolumeArea = 2001000 = 0.2 m
= 20 cm
0.2 m = 20 cm
