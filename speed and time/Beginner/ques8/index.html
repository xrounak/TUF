Given
Speed(foot) = 5 km/hr, Speed(bicycle) = 15 km/hr
Total distance = 40 km, Time = 5 hrs
Let foot distance = X km
Bicycle distance = (40 − X) km
Step 1
Time = DistanceSpeed
Walking: X5   Cycling: 40 − X15
X/5 + (40−X)/15 = 5
Step 2
LCM(5, 15) = 15
3X + (40 − X) = 75
2X + 40 = 75
Step 3
2X = 35
X = 17.5 km
