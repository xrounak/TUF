Step 2

3X − 2X60 = 2 ⇒ X60 = 2
X = 120 km
Step 3
Time = 12020 = 6 hours
Start time = 3 PM − 6 hrs = 9 AM
Step 4
9 AM → 2 PM = 5 hours
Speed = 1205 = 24 km/hr
