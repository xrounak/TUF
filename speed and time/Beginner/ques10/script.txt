Now in this problem, a train travels at 50 kilo-mee-ter per hour and reaches the station in 4 hours.

The question is asking us how much extra time the train would take if its speed were only 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

First, let us find the total distance.

Distance is equal to speed multiplied by time.

So, distance is equal to 50 multiplied by 4, which gives us 200 kilo-mee-ter.

Now we know the distance, so we can find the new time.

Time is equal to distance divided by speed.

So, the new time is 200 divided by 40, which is 5 hours.

But be careful here. The question is not asking for the new total time. It is asking how much "longer" the journey would take.

So, extra time is equal to 5 hours my-nus 4 hours, which is 1 hour.

Hence, the final answer is "1 hour", which is option B.
