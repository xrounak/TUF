800 m 8 min 20 s
Given
Speed = Distance / Time
Distance = 800 m
Time = 8 min 20 s
Step 1
Convert time into seconds
8 × 60 = 480 s
480 + 20 = 500 s
Step 2
Speed = DistanceTime
= 800500 = 1.6 m/s
Step 3
Convert m/s to km/hr
1 m/s = 3.6 km/hr
1.6 × 3.6 = 5.76 km/hr
