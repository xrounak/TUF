Two motorcycles, speed ratio 5 : 6. Motorcycle 2 covers 360 km in 3 hr. Find speed of Motorcycle 1.
5 : 6
Given
Ratio of speeds = 5 : 6
Speed 1 = 5x km/hr
Speed 2 = 6x km/hr
Distance = 360 km, Time = 3 hr
Step 1
Find actual speed of Motorcycle 2
Speed = Distance ÷ Time
= 360 ÷ 3
= 120 km/hr
Step 2
Compare 120 km/hr with ratio value 6x
6x = 120
x = 20
Step 3
Speed 1 = 5x
x = 20
= 5 × 20
= 100 km/hr
