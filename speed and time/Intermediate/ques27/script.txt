Now let us assume the total distance is D kilo-mee-ter.

Be very sure about one thing.

The starting time remains the same in all three cases.

Only the speed changes, so only the travel time changes.

Now let us use the formula.

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

So, the time taken in the first case is total distance divided by speed, which is D divided by 30.

And the time taken in the second case is total distance divided by speed, which is D divided by 40.

Now the difference between the two arrival times is 11 A.M. my-nus 9 A.M., which is 2 hours.

So we can write,

D divided by 30 my-nus D divided by 40 is equal to 2.

After solving this equation, we get,

D is equal to 240 kilo-mee-ter.

Now that we have found the total distance, we can find the starting time.

In the first case,

Time taken is equal to total distance divided by speed.

That is equal to 240 divided by 30.

This gives us 8 hours.

Since he reaches at 11 A.M.,

the starting time is 11 A.M. my-nus 8 hours.

That is equal to 3 A.M.

Now this part is completed.

We already know the starting time.

To reach at 10 A.M.,

the available travel time is from 3 A.M. to 10 A.M.

Required Speed is equal to Distance divided by Time.

Putting the values,

Required Speed is equal to 240 divided by 7.
