Speed and Time
Riding at 30 kmph, a man arrives at 11 A.M. At 40 kmph, he arrives at 9 A.M. To arrive at 10 A.M., his speed must be:
Q
🏍️
Riding at 30 kmph → 11 A.M., at 40 kmph → 9 A.M.. Speed for 10 A.M. arrival?
🏍️
Speed = ?
Given
Speed 1 = 30 km/h → Arrival = 11 AM
Speed 2 = 40 km/h → Arrival = 9 AM
Assume Total Distance = D km
Step 1
Time = DistanceSpeed
Time 1 = D30
Time 2 = D40
11 AM − 9 AM = 2 hours
D30 − D40 = 2
D = 240 km
Step 2
Time (Case 1) = 24030
= 8 hours
Start Time = 11 AM − 8 hrs
= 3 AM
Step 3
Available Time: 3 AM → 10 AM
= 7 hours
Required Speed = DistanceTime
= 2407
≈ 34.28 km/hr
