Speed and Time
A bus, without stoppage, covers a certain distance at a speed of 90 km/hr, and with stoppage, it covers the same distance at a speed of 75 km/hr. Find the stoppage time per hour.
Q
Bus: 90 km/hr without stoppage, 75 km/hr with stoppage. Find stoppage time per hour.
Without stoppage = 90 km/hr
With stoppage = 75 km/hr
Could have covered this distance
Given
Without stoppage = 90 km/hr
With stoppage = 75 km/hr
Moving time + Stoppage time → Avg. speed = 75 km/hr
Find: stoppage time / hr
Step 1
In 1 hour, without stoppage → 90 km
In 1 hour, with stoppage → 75 km
Step 2
Lost distance = 90 − 75 = 15 km
(not actually travelled — the bus could have covered it)
Time = Distance ÷ Speed
Stoppage time = 15 km ÷ 90 km/hr
= 1590 hr = 16 hr
Step 3
16 hr = 16 × 60 min
= 10 minutes
