Speed and Time
A man drives a distance. If he drives 5 km/hr faster, he saves 1 hour. If he drives 5 km/hr slower, he loses 2 hours. The distance is:
Q
+5 km/hr → −1 hr
−5 km/hr → +2 hr
Distance = ?
Drives 5 km/hr faster → saves 1 hr. Drives 5 km/hr slower → loses 2 hrs. Distance = ?
+5 km/hr → −1 hr
−5 km/hr → +2 hr
Given
Speed = X km/hr
Time = T hrs
Distance = X × T
Step 1
Speed increases by 5 → Time decreases by 1
New Speed = X + 5
New Time = T − 1
XT = (X + 5)(T − 1)
⇒ X = 5T − 5
Step 2
Speed decreases by 5 → Time increases by 2
New Speed = X − 5
New Time = T + 2
XT = (X − 5)(T + 2)
⇒ 2X = 5T + 10
Step 3
X = 5T − 5
Substitute into 2X = 5T + 10
⇒ T = 4 hrs
X = 5(4) − 5
⇒ X = 15 km/hr
Step 4
Distance = Speed × Time
Speed = 15 km/hr, Time = 4 hrs
Distance = 15 × 4
= 60 km
