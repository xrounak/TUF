Now in this problem, we are given that if a man drives 5 kilo-mee-ter per hour faster, he saves 1 hour.

And if he drives 5 kilo-mee-ter per hour slower, he loses 2 hours.

The question is asking us to find the total distance travelled.

Let the original speed be x kilo-mee-ter per hour.

And let the original time be t hours.

Since,

Distance is equal to Speed into Time,

the original distance is x into t.

Now let us use the first condition.

If the speed increases by 5, the time decreases by 1.

So,

New speed is equal to x pluss 5.

New time is equal to t my-nus 1.

So the new distance becomes (x pluss 5) into (t my-nus 1).

But remember, the distance remains the same.

So,

xt is equal to (x pluss 5)(t my-nus 1).

x is equal to 5t my-nus 5.

If the speed decreases by 5, the time increases by 2.

So,

New speed is x my-nus 5.

New time is t pluss 2.

Again, the distance remains the same.

So,

xt is equal to (x my-nus 5)(t pluss 2).

x is equal to 5t my-nus 5.
