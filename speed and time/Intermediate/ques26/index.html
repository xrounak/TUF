Average Speed = ?
Driver covers 150 km: first 75 km @ 25 km/hr, next 75 km @ 75 km/hr. Find average speed.
75 km @ 25 km/hr → 75 km @ 75 km/hr
Given
Average Speed = Total Distance ÷ Total Time
Distance 1 = 75 km, Speed 1 = 25 km/hr
Distance 2 = 75 km, Speed 2 = 75 km/hr
Time = Distance ÷ Speed
Step 1
Find time for first half
Time = 75 ÷ 25
= 3 hr
Step 2
Find time for second half
Time = 75 ÷ 75
= 1 hr
Step 3
Find Total Time
Total Time = Time 1 + Time 2
= 3 + 1
= 4 hr
Step 4
Total Distance = 150 km
Total Time = 4 hr
Average Speed = Total Distance ÷ Total Time
= 150 ÷ 4
= 37.5 km/hr
