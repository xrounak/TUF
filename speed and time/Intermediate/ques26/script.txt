Now in this problem, a driver completes a total distance of 150 kilo-mee-ter.

The trip is divided into two equal halves.

The first 75 kilo-mee-ter is covered at 25 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The next 75 kilo-mee-ter is covered at 75 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the "average speed" for the whole trip.

Be very sure about one thing.

Average speed is "not" the average of the two speeds.

So we must use the formula.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Now let us find the time taken for each half.

We know,

Time is equal to Distance divided by Speed.

For the first 75 kilo-mee-ter,

Time is equal to 75 divided by the speed during the first half, which is 25.

This gives us 3 hours.

Now that we know the time taken for the first half, let us find the time taken for the second half.

Time is equal to 75 divided by the speed during the second half, which is 75.

This gives us 1 hour.

Now this part is completed.

We already know the time taken for both halves, so let us find the total time.

Total Time is equal to the time taken to cover the first half pluss the time taken to cover the second half.

That is equal to 3 pluss 1.

This gives us 4 hours.

The total distance is already given.

which is 150 kilo-mee-ter.

Now we have everything we need.

Average Speed is equal to Total Distance divided by Total Time.

Putting the values,

Average Speed is equal to 150 divided by 4.

After simplifying this, we get 37.5 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the average speed of the driver is "37.5 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
