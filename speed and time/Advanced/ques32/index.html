Speed and Time
A car travelling at 60 kmph covers a journey in 2.5 hours. By how much must it increase its speed to cover the same journey in 1.5 hours?
Q
60 km/hr

Time: 1.5 hr
Car at 60 kmph: 2.5 hr → 1.5 hr. Find the speed increase.
Given
60 km/hr, 2.5 hr
Required
? km/hr, 1.5 hr
Given
Speed = 60 km/hr
Original Time = 2.5 hr
New Time = 1.5 hr
Step 1
Distance = Original Speed × Original Time
= 60 × 2.5
= 150 km
Same journey ⇒ distance unchanged
New Distance = 150 km
Step 2
New Speed = New Distance ÷ New Time
= 150 ÷ 1.5
= 100 km/hr
Step 3
Not the new speed — find the increase!
Increase = New Speed − Original Speed
= 100 − 60
= 40 km/hr
