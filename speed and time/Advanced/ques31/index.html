A train crosses a 2 km tunnel in 3 min 20 sec. Find speed in km/hr.
2 km
Given
Distance = 2 km
Time = 3 min 20 sec
Step 1
Convert 3 min 20 sec into hours
3 min = 3 × 60 = 180 sec
180 + 20 = 200 sec
1 hr = 3600 sec
200 = 2003600 hr
= 1/18 hr
Step 2
Speed = Distance / Time
= 2 / 118
= 36 km/hr
