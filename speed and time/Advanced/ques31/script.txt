Now in this problem, we are given that a train crosses a 2 kilo-mee-ter long tunnel in 3 minutes 20 seconds.

The question is asking us to find the speed of the train in kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So, the total distance covered is 2 kilo-mee-ter.

But the time is given in "minutes and seconds".

Since the speed is asked in kilo-mee-ter per hour, we first need to convert the time into "hours".

3 minutes 20 seconds means,

3 minutes is equal to 3 into 60, which is 180 seconds.

Adding the remaining 20 seconds,

we get 180 plus 20, which is 200 seconds.

Now we know that,

1 hour is equal to 3600 seconds.

So, to convert seconds into hours, we divide by 3600.

Therefore,

200 seconds is equal to 200 divided by 3600 hours.

After simplifying this, we get 1 by 18 hour.

Now we know the formula.

Speed is equal to Distance divided by Time.

Putting the values,

Speed is equal to 2 divided by 1 by 18.

After simplifying this, we get 36 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the speed of the train is "36 kilo-mee-ter per hour."
