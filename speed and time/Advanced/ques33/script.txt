Now in this problem, we are given that the speed of a delivery van excluding stops is 45 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Its average speed including stops is 36 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Also, the total journey time including stops is 5 hours.

The question is asking us to find the total stoppage time.

Now first, let us find the total distance covered.

Since the average speed including stops is 36 kilo-mee-ter per hour,

the total Distance is equal to Average Speed into Total Time.

That is equal to 36 into 5.

This gives us 180 kilo-mee-ter.

Now that we know the total distance, let us find the actual moving time.

Moving Time is equal to Total Distance divided by the speed at which the van was moving without any stoppage.

That is equal to 180 divided by 45.

This gives us 4 hours.

Now we have both the values we need.

The total journey took 5 hours, but the van was moving only for 4 hours.

So the remaining 1 hour must be the stoppage time.

Stoppage Time is equal to Total Time my-nus Moving Time.

That is equal to 5 my-nus 4.

This gives us 1 hour.

And 1 hour is equal to 60 minutes.

So the total stoppage time is "60 minutes."
