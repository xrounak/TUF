Now in this problem, a postman travelled total 65 kilo-mee-ter in 8 hours.
He did some part by walking, and some part by cycling.

Walking speed is 5 kilo-mee-ter per hour.
Cycling speed is 10 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the "cycling distance".

Let us take walking distance as x kilo-mee-ter.
So cycling distance will be 65 my-nus x kilo-mee-ter.

Now we know one basic formula.

Time is equal to distance divided by speed.

So time taken while walking will be x divided by 5.
And time taken while cycling will be 65 my-nus x, divided by 10.

Total time is given as 8 hours.
So we can write,

x by 5, pluss, 65 my-nus x by 10, is equal to 8.

Now take LCM as 10.
So this becomes,

2x pluss 65 my-nus x, divided by 10, is equal to 8.

After simplifying this, we get,

x pluss 65 is equal to 80.

So x is equal to 15.

That means walking distance is 15 kilo-mee-ter.

So cycling distance is 65 my-nus 15, which is 50 kilo-mee-ter.

Hence, the final answer will be option A, 50 kilo-mee-ter.
