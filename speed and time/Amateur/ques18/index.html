Step 1
Time = Distance ÷ Speed
Time (walk) = x/5
Time (cycle) = (65 − x)/10
x/5 + (65−x)/10 = 8
Step 2
[2x + 65 − x] /10 = 8
x + 65 = 80
Step 3
x = 15 km
