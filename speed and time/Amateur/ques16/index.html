Given
Distance 1 = 120 km, Speed 1 = 40 km/h
Distance 2 = 120 km, Speed 2 = 60 km/h
Step 1
Time = Distance ÷ Speed
First leg: 120 ÷ 40 = 3 hours
Step 2
Second leg: 120 ÷ 60 = 2 hours
Step 3
Total distance = 120 + 120 = 240 km
Total time = 5 hours
Average Speed = Total DistanceTotal Time
240 ÷ 5 = 48 km/h
