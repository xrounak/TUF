Now in this problem, a tourist travels 120 kilo-mee-ter at 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour.
Then the next 120 kilo-mee-ter is travelled at 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find the average speed for the total 240 kilo-mee-ter trip.

Now be careful here.
We should not directly average 40 and 60.
We should not say 40 plus 60 divided by 2 is 50.

Why?
Because average speed depends on "total distance" and "total time".
So first we need to find the time taken in both parts.

Now we know the formula.
Time is equal to distance divided by speed.

For the first 120 kilo-mee-ter, speed is 40.
So time is 120 divided by 40.
That gives us 3 hours.

For the second 120 kilo-mee-ter, speed is 60.
So time is 120 divided by 60.
That gives us 2 hours.

Now total distance is 120 plus 120, which is 240 kilo-mee-ter.
Total time is 3 plus 2, which is 5 hours.

Now average speed is equal to total distance divided by total time.

So average speed is 240 divided by 5.
That gives us 48 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So the final answer will be 48 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Hence, option C 48 kilo-mee-ter per hour is correct.
