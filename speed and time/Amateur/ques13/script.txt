Now in this problem, we are given that a train runs at 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour when stoppages are not included.
But when stoppages are included, the speed becomes 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

The question is asking us to find for how many minutes the train stops per hour.

Now let us understand what this means.

When stoppages are not included, the train is actually moving at 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour.
But when stoppages are included, the effective speed becomes 54 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So in one hour, instead of covering 72 kilo-mee-ter, the train effectively covers only 54 kilo-mee-ter.
This difference happens because the train stops for some time.

Got it? Perfect.

Now in 1 hour, at 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour, the train could have covered 72 kilo-mee-ter.

But because of stoppages, it covers only 54 kilo-mee-ter.

So the distance not covered due to stoppages is 72 my-nus 54.
That gives us 18 kilo-mee-ter.

Now this 18 kilo-mee-ter is the distance lost because the train was not moving.

So we need to find how much time the train would take to cover 18 kilo-mee-ter at its actual running speed.

The actual running speed is 72 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

So time lost will be distance divided by speed.
That means time lost will be 18 divided by 72 hours.

After simplifying this, we get 1 by 4 hour.

Now 1 by 4 hour means 15 minutes.

Hence, the train stops for "15 minutes per hour".
