Train runs 72 km/hr (no stop), 54 km/hr (with stop). Find stoppage time per hour.
Given
Speed (no stop) = 72 km/hr
Speed (with stop) = 54 km/hr
Step 1
Possible distance (1 hr) = 72 km
Actual distance (1 hr) = 54 km
72 − 54 = 18 km
Step 2
Distance lost = 18 km, Actual speed = 72 km/hr
Time lost = DistanceSpeed = 1872 = 1/4 hr = 15 min
