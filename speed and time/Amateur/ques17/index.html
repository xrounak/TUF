Alice travels by car — reaches C at 5 P.M. (40 kmph) or 1 P.M. (60 kmph). Speed to reach at 3 P.M.?
40 kmph → 5 P.M.
60 kmph → 1 P.M.
Given
Speed 1 = 40 kmph → 5 P.M.
Speed 2 = 60 kmph → 1 P.M.
Time difference = 4 hours
Step 1
Let t = time at 60 kmph
Time at 40 kmph = t + 4
Distance same in both cases
Distance = Speed × Time
At 60: Distance = 60 × t
At 40: Distance = 40 × (t + 4)
Since distance is same:
60t = 40(t + 4)
60t = 40t + 160
20t = 160
t = 8 hours
Step 2
Distance = 60 × 8 = 480 km
Start time = 1 P.M. − 8 hrs
= 5 A.M.
Step 3
5 A.M. → 3 P.M. = 10 hrs
Speed = 480 ÷ 10
= 48 kmph
