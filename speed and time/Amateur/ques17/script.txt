Now in this problem, Alice travels by car to reach point C.
If she travels at 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour, she reaches at 5 P.M.
If she travels at 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour, she reaches at 1 P.M.

The question is asking us to find the speed required to reach at 3 P.M.

Now first understand the time difference.
At 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour, she reaches at 5 P.M.
At 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour, she reaches at 1 P.M.

So the slower speed takes 4 hours more.
This is the main point.

Let the time taken at 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour be t hours.
Then the time taken at 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour will be t plus 4 hours.

Now distance is same in both cases.
So we can use,

distance is equal to speed into time.

At 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour , distance will be 60 into t.
At 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour , distance will be 40 into t plus 4.

Since distance is same, we can write,
60t is equal to 40 into t plus 4.

After simplifying this,
60t is equal to 40t plus 160.

So 20t is equal to 160.
That gives t equal to 8.

So time taken at 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour is 8 hours.

Now distance will be 60 into 8.
That is 480 kilo-mee-ter.

Now let us find the starting time.
At 60 kilo-mee-ter per hour, Alice reaches at 1 P.M.
And the journey time is 8 hours.

So starting time is 1 P.M. minus 8 hours.
That gives 5 A.M.

Now she wants to reach at 3 P.M.
From 5 A.M. to 3 P.M., total time is 10 hours.

So required speed is distance divided by time.
That is 480 divided by 10.

So required speed is 48 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

hence the final answer will be 48 kilo-mee-ter per hour.
