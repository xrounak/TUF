Now in this problem, a bus travels at 80 kilo-mee-ter per hour for 6 hours.

The same distance must be covered in only 4.5 hours.

What do we need to find?
We need to find the new speed of the bus.

First, let us find the distance covered by the bus.

Distance is equal to speed multiplied by time.

So, distance is equal to 80 multiplied by 6, which gives us 480 kilo-mee-ter.

Now that we know the distance, we can find the required speed.

Speed is equal to distance divided by time.

So, the required speed is 480 divided by 4.5.

After solving this, we get 106.67 kilo-mee-ter per hour.

Hence, the final answer is "106.67 kilo-mee-ter per hour", which is option C.
