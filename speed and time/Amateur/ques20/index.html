Alex drives 50 km/h for 2h, then 40 km/h for 3h. Total distance?
50 km/h · 2h 40 km/h · 3h
Given
Speed₁ = 50 km/h
Time₁ = 2h
Speed₂ = 40 km/h
Time₂ = 3h
Distance = Speed × Time
Step 1
Distance in first part
Distance₁ = 50 × 2 = 100 km
Step 2
Distance in second part
Distance₂ = 40 × 3 = 120 km
Step 3
Total distance = 100 km + 120 km
100 + 120 = 220 km
