Now in this problem, alex is travelling in two parts.
In the first part, he drives at 50 kilo-mee-ter per hour for 2 hours.
In the second part, because of traffic, he drives at 40 kilo-mee-ter per hour for 3 hours.

The question is asking us to find the "total distance" travelled by alex.

Now we know one simple formula.

Distance equal to speed into time.

So first, let us find the distance in the first part.
in first part Speed is 50, and time is 2 hours.

So distance will be 50 into 2, which is 100 kilo-mee-ter.


Next,  find the distance in the second part.
now in second part Speed is 40, and time is 3 hours.

So distance will be 40 into 3, which is 120 kilo-mee-ter.

Now we have both distances.
in First part it is  100 kilo-mee-ter.
and in Second part it's 120 kilo-mee-ter.

Since the question asks for total distance, we need to add both.

100 pluss 120 equal to 220 kilo-mee-ter.

So the final answer will be 220 kilo-mee-ter.
